We use the information we collect for the following purposes.

  4. Preventing fraudulent use and the unauthorized unlocking of paid features through device tampering
  5. Investigating and improving malfunctions of the App
  6. Complying with this Policy, the Terms of Service, and the disclosure based on the Specified Commercial Transactions Act

3. Where Information Is Stored

3-1. Information stored only on your device

As a rule, the following information is stored only on your device (SharedPreferences) and is not sent to our servers.

  • Name, date of birth, time of birth, place of birth
  • Current base information
  • Display history of reading results
  • App settings

3-2. Information sent to servers or third-party services

Among the App's features, fraud prevention, generation of reading interpretations by Stella, and subscription management send the following information to third-party services over the internet.

  • Device attestation information (App Attest / Play Integrity): For fraud prevention (device attestation), this is sent to Apple Inc.'s App Attest service (iOS edition) and Google LLC's Play Integrity service (Android edition). It contains only an anonymous device identifier and app-launch information, and does not include your personal information or reading results.
  • Astrological calculation results (planetary positions, geographic coordinates, consultation prompts, etc.): To generate interpretations by Stella, tarot interpretations, star readings, and relocation interpretations, these are sent via our server (Cloudflare Workers) to the Gemini API provided by Google LLC. The information sent includes the consultation text you enter, birth information (date of birth, time of birth, place of birth), astrological data based on your natal chart, and the geographic coordinates you select. After being used to generate interpretations, this information is handled in accordance with Google's terms so that it is not retained on the Gemini API side.
  • Subscription identifiers (Apple ID / Google Play ID / Sign-in ID) and purchase information: For billing management, these are sent to the subscription-management service RevenueCat, Inc., and used to verify and restore purchase status. Identifiers are treated as anonymous IDs.
  • Map tile requests: To display the map screen, requests are sent via our server (Cloudflare Workers) to the tile server of OpenStreetMap (OSM Humanitarian). No personal information is included.
Our server retention policy:
The Cloudflare Workers server we operate does not permanently store the content described above. Aside from a cache of subscription identifiers (up to 5 minutes) and an edge cache of map tiles (up to 24 hours), no personal information or reading results are recorded on our servers.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties except in the following cases.

  1. When you have consented
  2. When there is a disclosure request based on law (court order, inquiry from an investigative authority, etc.)
  3. When we entrust information to the following third-party services to the extent necessary to provide the App's features:
    • Apple Inc. (device attestation via App Attest [iOS edition]; subscription processing via StoreKit [iOS edition])
    • Google LLC (device attestation via Play Integrity [Android edition]; subscription processing via Google Play Billing [Android edition]; generation of reading-interpretation text via the Gemini API [Stella consultations, tarot, star readings, relocation interpretations])
    • RevenueCat, Inc. (management of subscription status)
    • Cloudflare, Inc. (API relay via Workers and map-tile delivery)
    • OpenStreetMap Foundation / Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (provision of map tiles)

5. Deletion of Data

You can delete data related to the App in the following ways.

  • Deleting all in-app data: Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on your device. However, if iCloud backup on iOS or Google Drive backup on Android is enabled, some data may be retained as a cloud backup according to those settings. To delete the backup completely, please delete the backup separately from each cloud service's settings.
  • Unlinking sign-in: You can sign out from the Account section of the Sanctuary screen. Signing out removes the link between this device and RevenueCat.
  • Canceling your subscription: On iOS, cancel from the "Settings" app → Apple ID → Subscriptions; on Android, cancel from the Google Play Store menu → Subscriptions.
  • Request to delete personal information: As a rule, we do not retain your personal information on our servers (see Section 3). If you wish to delete any information that may remain with our partner services (such as RevenueCat), please contact us at the address in Section 9.
